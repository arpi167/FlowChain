# Business Requirement Document (BRD)

# FlowChain
### AI-Powered Supply Chain Intelligence Platform

---

# 1. Introduction

FlowChain is an enterprise-grade Supply Chain Intelligence Platform developed to demonstrate how SQL, Excel, Power BI, Python, and AI can be integrated to solve real-world supply chain and business intelligence problems.

The platform focuses on procurement, inventory management, warehouse operations, logistics, supplier performance, and executive decision support using publicly available datasets and realistic business scenarios.

---

# 2. Project Overview

FlowChain aims to provide a centralized analytics platform capable of transforming operational supply chain data into actionable business insights.

The project demonstrates the complete Business Intelligence lifecycle, including data modeling, SQL analytics, Excel-based operational reporting, Power BI dashboards, and AI-assisted decision support.

---

# 3. Problem Statement

Organizations generate large volumes of operational data across procurement, inventory, logistics, warehouses, and suppliers. In many cases, this information is distributed across multiple systems, making it difficult to monitor business performance and support timely decision-making.

FlowChain addresses this challenge by integrating supply chain data into a centralized platform that enables business users to monitor key performance indicators, analyze operational performance, and receive AI-driven recommendations for better decision-making.

---

# 4. Objectives

The primary objectives of FlowChain are:

- Design a normalized relational database for supply chain operations.
- Perform advanced SQL-based business analysis.
- Build Excel models for operational planning.
- Develop interactive Power BI dashboards.
- Generate business KPIs for executive reporting.
- Integrate AI-powered decision support.
- Demonstrate an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution.

---

# 7. Functional Requirements

The platform shall:

- Store master and transactional supply chain data.
- Maintain supplier information.
- Track inventory levels.
- Monitor warehouse utilization.
- Analyze purchase orders.
- Track shipments and deliveries.
- Measure supplier performance.
- Generate executive dashboards.
- Provide AI-powered recommendations.

---

# 8. Non-Functional Requirements
